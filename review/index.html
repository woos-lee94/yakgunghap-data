App Review demo materials

약궁합 (Yakgunghap) · Sample images for testing the barcode and pharmacy-bag features. They contain no real patient or pharmacy information.

1. Box barcode (상자 바코드)

Show this page on another screen (for example a Mac) and scan it with the review device.

  1. In the app, tap "약 추가" (Add) on the "내 약통" tab, then "상자 바코드" (Box barcode).
  2. On the camera explanation screen tap "계속" (Continue) and choose whether to allow camera access in the system prompt.
  3. Point the camera at barcode 1. The app opens 명인피모짓정4밀리그램; tap "이 약이 맞아요 — 내 약통에 넣기" (Add to my medicines).
  4. Do the same with barcode 2 (부광미졸렌정10밀리그램(미졸라스틴)). The home screen then shows a red "위험 1" (Danger 1) contraindication.
  5. Barcode 3 is a health supplement (고려홍삼분말캡슐, red ginseng).

2. Pharmacy bag / prescription (약봉투·처방전)

The easiest way is to pick the sample from Photos, so no second screen is needed.

  1. On the review device, open this page in Safari, touch and hold the sample below and tap "Save to Photos" (or download sample-medication-guide.png).
  2. In the app, tap "약 추가" (Add), then "약봉투·처방전" (Pharmacy bag / prescription), then "앨범에서 고르기" (Choose from album) and select the sample. (Or tap "사진 찍기" (Take photo) and photograph it on another screen.)
  3. The app reads the text on the device, skips the lines that look like personal information (name, date, pharmacy) and preselects the two medicines. Tap "고른 2개 내 약통에 넣기" (Add the 2 selected to my medicines).
  4. The home screen shows the same contraindication as above. The photo copy is deleted right after reading.
